# BiSell AutoIQ

Package: `zw.co.bisell.autoiq.mobile`

Category: Auto & Vehicles

Short description (74 characters):

> Buy and sell vehicles with verified details, requests, and viewings.

Full description:

> BiSell AutoIQ is a Zimbabwe-focused vehicle marketplace that keeps the facts,
> requests, and next steps in one place.
>
> Buyers can browse published vehicles, filter by make, model, year, location,
> body type, and verification status, save vehicles, request quotes, source a
> specific vehicle, and arrange viewings.
>
> Sellers can create and manage listings, add vehicle specifications, pricing,
> photos, and ownership documents, submit vehicles for review, and respond to
> viewing activity.
>
> Where available, inspection and verification information is shown with the
> listing so users can understand the available context before making contact.
>
> AutoIQ keeps marketplace activity structured from search to handover.
